When is the evidence enough?

Pharma rarely lacks evidence. The problem is that evidence often becomes conclusive only after the market has already changed — far too late for us to act. Meanwhile, our competitors have got one step ahead of us while our teams are still building a case.

The strongest leadership preserves rigour, while recognising when the available evidence is already sufficient to take action… or not.

The most costly decisions rarely look foolish at the time. Being stuck just looks like a pause at first. Sometimes, the most capable teams get into that position despite being surrounded by respected experts, credible research and good information. And the first signs might appear across different functions, markets and conversations, long before they form an undeniable pattern.

In this fireside conversation, Yariv Hefez, Former SVP Oncology, Merck, and Erin Mistry, CEO of Within3, will rewind real decisions made under uncertainty. They’ll examine what was visible at the time, what remained ambiguous, which signals deserved greater attention and when a different course was still possible. They’ll also explore how leaders can build a more connected and continuously updated view of the market.
