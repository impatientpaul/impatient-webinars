Starts in

Pharma advertising was always about more than delivering information.

We helped people understand, we made ideas stick, we gave reasons to take the next step.

Now AI has got in on the act.

Patients and HCPs are now asking a question, interrogating the evidence, comparing treatments and getting a point of view before even encountering your campaign. Yes, GEO can help ensure your brand is visible inside that AI answer. But visibility isn’t preference, and being accurately summarised by a machine isn’t the same as being chosen.

This isn’t theory. Pew’s August 2026 survey found 34% of US adults use AI chatbots for at least one health-related purpose, including 22% looking for treatment information and 25% trying to understand symptoms. Almost all users found the health information at least “somewhat helpful”.

So what is advertising for now? Indeed, what are our marketing jobs for now?

Technology can be destructive. Nobody uses the Yellow Pages anymore. But it’s also always constructive too. Technology didn’t kill the need for businesses to be noticed and appreciated. AI may do something similar to pharma marketing, because if information becomes easier to find, compare and interrogate, creativity has to earn its value somewhere else. It has to make something matter, make it memorable, and create a preference.
