When is the evidence enough?

Pharma rarely lacks evidence. The problem is that evidence often becomes conclusive only after the market has already changed — far too late for us to act.

The strongest leadership preserves rigour, while recognising when the available evidence is already sufficient to take action… or not.

In this fireside conversation, Yariv Hefez and Erin Mistry rewind real decisions made under uncertainty. They examine what was visible at the time, which signals deserved greater attention and when a different course was still possible.
